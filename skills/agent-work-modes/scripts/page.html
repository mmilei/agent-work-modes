Ways of working with coding agents

Two layers. Forms of work are picked per task and answer "how do I attack this?". Principles are not picked: they sit under every form and explain why it works and where it breaks.

Pick one per task. Some add on top of another form, one runs between tasks.

Layer 2 · always on
